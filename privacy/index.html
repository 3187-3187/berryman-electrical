Privacy

How website enquiries are handled.

Website enquiries, including any photos or PDFs you choose to attach, are processed through Cloudflare and sent to Berryman Electrical using the Resend email service. You can also contact us directly by phone or email.

Information the website may collect

An online enquiry can include your name, contact details, suburb, job description and any photos or PDF documents you choose to attach.

Why it is collected

Information supplied through an enquiry is intended to be used to respond to the enquiry, understand the requested electrical work, arrange work where applicable and maintain ordinary business records.

Photos

Only attach photos or PDFs that are relevant to the electrical enquiry. Do not upload passwords, access codes, identity documents or sensitive information. Attachments are emailed to the business through Resend and may be retained in business email records.

Access and correction

For privacy questions relating to Berryman Electrical, contact info@berrymanelectrical.com.au.

Online enquiry services: Cloudflare Pages processes online form submissions, Cloudflare Turnstile performs an anti-spam check, and Resend handles email delivery to our business inbox. Submitted information and attachments may be kept in business email records. To ask about information you have submitted, email info@berrymanelectrical.com.au.
