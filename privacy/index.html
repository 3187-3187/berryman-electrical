Privacy

How website enquiries are handled.

This staging build does not transmit enquiry-form data. The final production policy will be confirmed alongside the form and email systems before launch.

Information the website may collect

When the production enquiry form is connected, an enquiry may include your name, contact details, suburb, job description and any photos you choose to provide.

Why it is collected

Information supplied through an enquiry is intended to be used to respond to the enquiry, understand the requested electrical work, arrange work where applicable and maintain ordinary business records.

Photos

Only upload photos that you are comfortable providing for the purpose of assessing the electrical enquiry. Avoid sending passwords, access codes, identity documents or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Access and correction

For privacy questions relating to Berryman Electrical, contact info@berrymanelectrical.com.au.
