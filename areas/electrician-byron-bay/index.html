Coastal exposure

Salt, moisture and weather can matter for outdoor enclosures, fittings and equipment. Visible corrosion or water staining deserves proper assessment.

Older and altered wiring

Businesses and trading hours

New electrical loads

EV charging, air conditioning, induction cooking and renovation loads can justify checking switchboard space, protection and supply capacity.

Diagnosis first

Find the cause before replacing parts.

A safety switch or breaker that keeps tripping is telling you something happened; it does not tell you the cause. Fault finding narrows the problem logically so the repair is based on evidence rather than guesswork.
