Do you provide electrical services in Mullumbimby?

Can you find out why a circuit or safety switch keeps tripping?

Yes. An unknown electrical fault generally needs inspection and testing before the cause and repair scope can be established. Repeated resets are not a substitute for diagnosis.

Can you add power to a shed or outbuilding?

We can assess new supply and circuit requirements, including distance, cable route, loads, protection and switchboard capacity. Scope and any required trenching or other works are established before quoting.

Do all older Mullumbimby switchboards need replacing?

No. Age alone does not determine the work required. Board condition, circuit protection, access and the proposed electrical loads are assessed before an upgrade is recommended.
