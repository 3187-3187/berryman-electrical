From the real work archive

Renovations reveal what was hidden.

Once wall linings come off, older cabling, previous alterations and awkward routes can become visible. Planning the electrical work early reduces surprises later.

Common questions.

When should the electrician get involved in a renovation?

Early enough that power, lighting, switching and equipment locations can be planned before finishes and cabinetry make changes harder.

Can you work from plans and then adjust on site?

Yes. Plans are useful, but final electrical positions often benefit from a practical site review.

Can switchboard work be included in the renovation?

Yes. If the renovation changes the electrical demand or circuit arrangement, the board should be considered as part of the project.
