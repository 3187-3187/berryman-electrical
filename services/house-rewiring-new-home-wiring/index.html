Specialist services

Wiring projects that need clear planning

Unlike everyday electrical repairs, rewiring and new-build work often involve multiple trades, wall or ceiling access and staged testing.

Full house rewiring

Assess ageing or unsuitable existing circuits and develop a replacement scope based on access and the home's layout.

Partial rewiring

Replace or extend defined sections of wiring during upgrades, repairs, kitchen or bathroom renovation and alterations.

New home rough-in and fit-off

Plan circuits, outlets, switches, lighting and fixed-appliance connections through appropriate building stages.

Switchboard and protection planning

Review circuit capacity, protective devices, earthing and the condition of the existing board alongside the proposed wiring.

Lighting, power and future loads

Allow for practical switching, external power, heat-pump or EV requirements, data outlets and additional dedicated circuits.

Renovation coordination

Work around wall access, other trades, structural constraints, finishes and safe temporary power arrangements.

Before booking

What we check before the work starts.

Access to cables

Existing installation

Testing and inspection may reveal legacy wiring or circuits that need a broader scope than first expected.

Plans and room layout

Testing and certificates

Answers before you enquire

Common questions about house rewiring & new home electrical wiring.

Can you rewire an older home while it is occupied?

Possibly, depending on access, circuit layout and safe isolation arrangements. We scope disruption and temporary power requirements before the job.

Do you do new build electrical rough-ins?

Yes. We can discuss new residential wiring projects, rough-in and fit-off in coordination with the construction schedule.

Will the switchboard need replacing too?

Not necessarily, but the existing protection, capacity and condition should be assessed as part of a rewiring project.

Can the wiring be replaced without removing wall linings?

Some routes may be accessible through roof or floor spaces; other sections can require building access and subsequent patching by the relevant trade.

Can data outlets be included?

Yes, subject to the relevant cabling registration and competencies. Combining electrical and communications planning can help during an open-wall stage.

Do you provide electrical compliance documentation?

Yes. Applicable electrical installation work is tested and NSW CCEW documentation is submitted as required.
