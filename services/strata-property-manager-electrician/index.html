Specialist services

Electrical support for managed properties

Strata and property work often means coordinating access, occupants, committees and approvals as carefully as the electrical repair itself.

Common-area lighting

Failed LED battens, stairwell and corridor lights, exterior and sensor lighting, timers and shared-area illumination.

Switchboards and safety devices

Investigate reported tripping, protection issues and ageing distribution equipment; scope required repairs and upgrades.

Rental property electrical repairs

Powerpoints, lights, fans, intermittent faults and damaged accessories, with access arranged through an authorised agent.

Smoke alarm electrical work

Hard-wired alarm installation, testing and electrical documentation. Wider statutory certification is coordinated with appropriately accredited partners where needed.

Commercial tenancies

Electrical supply faults, maintenance and small alterations in mixed-use and commercial buildings.

Planned maintenance and minor projects

Group approved tasks where suitable, plan access and shutdowns, and identify work requiring a separate quote.

Before booking

What we check before the work starts.

Who authorises the work?

We identify the responsible manager or owner and distinguish fault-finding attendance from work that needs a committee decision or quote.

Occupants and access

Share building access, tenancy contacts, permitted attendance windows and any site rules before booking.

Urgency and hazards

Loss of common lighting, exposed conductors, repeated trips and other safety concerns may need phone-first triage.

Clear handover

Record the work completed, observations and recommended next steps, including applicable testing and electrical documentation.

How the work is organised

Four practical steps.

01

Send the job or maintenance list

Identify the property suburb, what has failed, the site contact, photographs taken safely and how access is organised.

02

Triage and agree scope

Discuss whether diagnosis is required, whether access or shutdowns will affect occupants, and who approves additional work.

03

Attend and investigate

Carry out authorised electrical assessment and agreed repairs or installations within the available access window.

04

Report findings and follow-ups

Explain the work and testing performed, any outstanding risks and the next practical step for the manager.

Useful first message: the site suburb, the issue or proposed job, safe photos and any essential access or timing details. We can then discuss the right assessment or quote pathway.
Answers before you enquire

Common questions about strata & property manager electrical services.

Do you work for strata committees and property management agencies?

Yes. We take enquiries from authorised strata managers, real estate agents and facility managers for common-area and property electrical work.

Can you attend multiple properties?

We can discuss grouped maintenance and scheduled visits based on location, access and the approved work list.

Will you communicate with tenants directly?

Access can be coordinated with the nominated manager or authorised site contact, using the agreed communication process.

Can you handle after-hours faults?

We can discuss urgent electrical call-outs by phone. Attendance and timing depend on workload, location and the nature of the fault.

Can you arrange smoke alarm certification?

We provide electrical installation testing and applicable electrical compliance documentation; wider statutory fire-safety sign-off may need accredited partners.

What details are needed to start?

The suburb, site contact, scope, work authorisation, relevant access times and safe photographs help us triage the request.
