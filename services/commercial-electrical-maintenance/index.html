What we do

Electrical work we can help with.

Each installation or fault is assessed according to its condition, equipment and required outcome.

Electrical fault finding

Investigate dead circuits, repeated tripping, lost power and intermittent electrical problems.

Lighting and power maintenance

Replace failed fittings, assess LEDs, maintain switches, outlets, external lighting and everyday electrical items.

Switchboard and protection work

Assess board condition, protective devices, labels and faults before recommending improvements.

Equipment feeds and controls

Investigate electrical supplies, isolators, controls and starters within the agreed work scope.

Planned maintenance visits

Combine suitable maintenance items, prioritise repairs and coordinate access where practical.

Tenancy and workplace alterations

Power, lighting and distribution changes for refits, changed layouts and equipment upgrades.

Getting the scope right

What we consider before starting.

Trading times and shutdowns

Identify which areas must remain operational and suitable access or power-isolation windows.

Fault history

Previous repairs, symptoms, photos and equipment models can help identify intermittent problems.

Building access

Clarify keys, tenancy or manager approval, inductions, permits and site contacts in advance.

Agreed work scope

Separate investigative diagnostic work from clearly defined repairs and any specialist equipment servicing.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions.

Can you maintain a café or shop?

Yes. We take enquiries for appropriate electrical maintenance and repairs across commercial premises.

Can you work outside trading hours?

Possibly. We confirm available times and access requirements before booking; after-hours attendance is not guaranteed.

Do you work with facility or strata managers?

Yes, when appropriate work authorisation and access arrangements are in place.

Do you repair all commercial appliances?

We assess relevant electrical supplies and electrical faults. Internal repairs to specialist appliances may require their technician.

Can we schedule planned maintenance?

Yes, the appropriate scope and frequency can be discussed according to the premises and equipment.
