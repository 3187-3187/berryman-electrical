Getting the scope right

What we consider before starting.

Model and age

Identify alarm brands and model labels from safe photos, without opening electrical connections.

Placement and coverage

The building layout and current requirements determine minimum positions; Fire and Rescue NSW recommends wider coverage.

Compatibility

Interconnected units and mounting arrangements may require suitable matching replacements rather than a simple base swap.

Rental responsibilities

NSW landlords must ensure smoke alarms work, inspect them as required and arrange prompt repair or replacement.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions.

Can I change a hard-wired smoke alarm myself?

Hard-wired smoke alarm installation and replacement must be done by a licensed electrician.

When is replacement required?

NSW rental properties generally require alarm replacement within 10 years of manufacture or sooner if the manufacturer specifies or the unit fails.

Are smoke alarms required in rentals?

Yes. NSW landlords have duties concerning working smoke alarms, checks and timely repairs, with some specific strata arrangements.

Will a new alarm fit the old base?

Not always. Electrical connections, model, mounting and interconnection all need to be checked.

Should every bedroom have an alarm?

Fire and Rescue NSW recommends alarms in bedrooms and living areas beyond the legal minimum.
