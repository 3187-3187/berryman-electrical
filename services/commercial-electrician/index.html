Common questions

Commercial electrical FAQs.

Do you provide electrical work for shops, cafés and business premises?

Yes. Berryman Electrical accepts commercial electrical enquiries for faults, maintenance, lighting, power and planned changes, depending on job scope and availability.

Can you investigate a failed circuit or piece of equipment?

Yes, where electrical diagnosis falls within the work scope. We start with symptoms and suitable testing. Specialist appliance or equipment repairs may require the equipment's own service technician.

Can electrical work be planned around trading hours?

We can discuss access, shutdowns and suitable times when scoping the work. Specific times are agreed according to the job and availability rather than guaranteed on the website.

Can you replace fluorescent lighting with LEDs?

Yes. We can assess existing fittings and wiring, suitable replacements, mounting and access. Removal and disposal requirements are included in the agreed scope where relevant.

Can you assess a commercial switchboard?

Yes. We can review the board condition, protection, labelling, circuits and potential upgrade requirements. Metering or network-side work may need separate coordination.

How do commercial fault-finding charges work?

An unknown electrical problem may require charged diagnostic attendance before repairs can be scoped. We confirm the current minimum, subsequent labour, travel and GST treatment before booking where applicable.
