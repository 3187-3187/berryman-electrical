What we check before the work starts.

Registration and competencies

Customer cabling is performed by a registered cabler with the category and additional competencies required for the particular work.

Network boundaries

Carrier/NBN-owned or network-side equipment and faults may need the carrier or an authorised service technician.

Cable planning and separation

Cabling advice and records

Answers before you enquire

Common questions about data & communications cabling.

Are you registered to install data cabling?

Yes. Berryman Electrical holds cabling registration and undertakes work within the applicable registration category and competencies.

Can you run CAT6 cable to a home office?

We can assess Ethernet outlet and copper cabling installations where the building access and our registration scope permit.

Do you install both power and data for office fit-outs?

We can discuss the electrical and customer communications cabling together while keeping each work type within its relevant requirements.

Can you modify my NBN network connection?

Customer-owned cabling and carrier-owned network equipment are different. Network-side changes may require your internet provider or the NBN-authorised technician.

Will you supply a cabling compliance form?

Registered cabling work is carried out to applicable rules, and we provide the required telecommunications cabling advice documentation.

Can you diagnose slow internet?

We can assess relevant customer data cabling and terminations. ISP service issues, router configuration and provider infrastructure faults may need a separate network specialist.
