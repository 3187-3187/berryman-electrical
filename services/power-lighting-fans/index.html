Help us scope the job

Four details that make electrical quotes easier.

You do not need a technical diagnosis. A clear description of the number of items, where they belong and what is there now makes a useful start.

How many items?

List each new or replacement fitting, fan, switch or powerpoint, including how many at each location.

Exact locations

Describe rooms and proposed fitting positions, and provide safe photos or a marked-up plan where useful.

Existing vs new

Tell us if we are replacing something already wired, moving it, or supplying a completely new point or circuit.

Access & equipment

Mention ceiling height, roof access, construction type, preferred fixtures, dimming/smart-control needs and any supplied products.

A useful starting point

Send a simple numbered job list.

For example: two new double powerpoints in the study, three LED downlights in the kitchen and one replacement ceiling fan on the verandah.

Common decisions

Choose fittings that suit the installation.

LED colour and dimming

Warm, neutral and cool light create different results. For dimming, the control and fittings need to be compatible, and the circuit may need assessment.

Ceiling fans and mounting

Secure support, clearance, wiring access, switching and the selected fan's installation requirements matter as much as appearance.

Outdoor lighting

Weather and moisture exposure affect fitting choice, mounting, connections and the protection required. Coastal exposure can also affect durability.

Additional circuits

Some new outlets or equipment may require more than a convenient connection point. We consider the existing circuit, load and protective devices.

Common questions

Power, lighting and fan FAQs.

Can you install extra powerpoints in an existing home?

Yes. We can assess a new outlet's location, existing circuit arrangement, protection and the practical cable route before confirming the work required.

Can old light fittings be replaced with LEDs?

Yes. Existing fittings can often be replaced or upgraded, subject to wiring condition, mounting and suitability. We can discuss colour temperature, light levels and any dimmer compatibility.

Do you install ceiling fans or replace existing fans?

Yes. New and replacement fan jobs depend on mounting support, existing wiring, access and the selected fan and controller. An existing light point does not automatically mean a fan can be fitted without additional work.

Can you install lights outdoors or in wet areas?

Yes, subject to the site's conditions and applicable electrical requirements. Equipment location, weather exposure and suitability need checking, particularly for external areas and bathrooms.

Can I combine several small lighting and power jobs?

Yes. Send a numbered list of each item, its quantity and location. Safe photos help identify the materials and access requirements before scheduling.

What happens if a new LED light keeps tripping or flickering?

Flickering or repeated protective-device trips may involve wiring, connections, controls or equipment compatibility. Where the cause is uncertain, fault finding is the appropriate first step rather than guessing which fitting to replace.

Do you supply the fittings or can I buy my own?

We can discuss either approach. Any owner-supplied item must be appropriate for its location and installation, with the necessary instructions and approvals. Installation suitability is confirmed before work is agreed.
