Getting the scope right

What we consider before starting.

Electrical or plumbing?

No hot water can have several causes. Water leaks, pressure and valve issues are normally plumbing matters.

Make and model

The rating plate and equipment type help assess the system and determine suitable testing.

Controlled supply

Off-peak and timed supplies may mean power is not present continuously; timing details matter.

Safety first

Repeated tripping, burning smells or moisture near electrical parts should be assessed rather than repeatedly reset.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions.

Can an electrician repair electric hot water?

An electrician can assess and repair the relevant electrical circuits and components within licence and equipment scope. Plumbing requires a plumber.

Why does the hot-water circuit trip?

Possible causes include equipment faults, leakage or damaged wiring; proper testing is needed to identify the cause.

Could off-peak electricity be the issue?

Possibly. The control and supply arrangements must be checked before blaming the heater.

Will it be fixed in one visit?

Not necessarily. That depends on the diagnosis, safe access, available parts and whether the fault is electrical.

What should I send?

The suburb, symptoms, model details, whether breakers trip and safe photos of visible equipment.
