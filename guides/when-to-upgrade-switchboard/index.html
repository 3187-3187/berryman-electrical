Electrical guide

When should a switchboard be upgraded?

A switchboard does not need replacing simply because it is old, but age, limited protection, crowding and new electrical loads are common reasons to have it assessed.

Short answer: Common triggers include porcelain fuses, limited safety-switch protection, a crowded board, repeated alterations, new high-load equipment or a renovation that adds circuits.

What can be involved.

Older protection

Porcelain fuses or older protective devices are a common reason to review the board.

Crowding

Years of additions can leave a board difficult to work on or poorly arranged.

New electrical loads

EV charging, renovations and added equipment can change what the board needs to support.

Poor labelling

Clear circuit identification matters for future fault finding and maintenance.

Safety note: This guide is general information, not DIY electrical instructions. Electrical testing, repairs and alterations should be carried out by an appropriately licensed electrician.
